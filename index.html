Professional Projects

There are some of the projects I have worked on professionally, along with a large team of other Designers, Developers, and Artists. These are games that are currently live in front of thousands of players and can be found in the mobile game stores.

Dragon Story is a casual game about collecting, breeding, and raising dragons. My responsibilities include designing and implementing in-game LiveOps events, including the visual design of dragons and environments. Much of the work involves using spreadsheets to structure and format data for import into an SQL database. I collaborate closely with a team of artists and release managers to coordinate the development, preparation, and deployment of these events.

Castle Story is a casual game about building and managing a medieval kingdom. My responsibilities include designing and implementing in-game narrative events, including creating quest chains and writing supporting storylines. I also contribute to the visual design of characters and environments, as well as game economy design. A significant part of my work involves using spreadsheets to structure and format stories, quests, and other game data for import into an SQL database. I collaborate closely with a team of artists and release managers to coordinate the development, preparation, and deployment of these events.

Imperia Online is a MMORTS focused on building an empire, managing resources, and conquering rival kingdoms. My responsibilities involve designing and balancing new multiplayer game tournaments and game modes, as well as improving and refining existing ones. I work closely with a team of developers and product managers to ensure that new features and gameplay experiences are well-designed, balanced, and aligned with the overall product vision.

Fantasy Forest Story is a casual game about collecting, breeding, and raising magical creatures. My responsibilities involve scheduling, coordinating, and releasing thematic events throughout the year. I manage and format event data for import into an SQL database using spreadsheets. I primarily work on this game independently.

Crush Them All is an idle RPG about collecting heroes, upgrading them, and defeating waves of enemies. My responsibilities included designing and balancing various game modes and mini-games, as well as overhauling and improving existing game mechanics and implementing quality-of-life improvements. I worked on Midgard Heroes: Ragnarok Idle, a game derived from Crush Them All, collaborating closely with my mentor, to refine and improve the overall gameplay experience.

Siege: World War II is a real-time tactical strategy game where players collect and deploy historical military units to outmaneuver their opponents. During my brief involvement with the game, I designed and balanced several recurring events, working closely with my mentor and the product manager to ensure they were engaging, well-balanced, and aligned with the game’s overall direction.

Personal Projects

These are some of the projects I have worked on by myself, or with small teams of university classmates. These are small projects that I have developed to improve my skills. Playable Prototypes can be found their respective Itch pages.

Perplexity is a split-screen cooperative puzzle game where two players work together to escape through levels representing different stages of the Internet’s development. My responsibilities included level design, puzzle design, and 3D modelling, with a focus on creating challenges that make use of the game’s split-screen perspective. I also designed and built physical Pillow Switches using Arduino, which allow the game to be quickly reset between players during exhibition use. I collaborated with a multidisciplinary team to develop and prepare the game in Unity for exhibition at Overkill Festival 2023.

Rankle is a casual puzzle game where players rank the ten most popular answers to a daily question. My responsibilities included designing and implementing the game’s UI/UX in the Defold Engine, ensuring that the interface was intuitive and supported the core gameplay experience. I also served as the team lead, coordinating the development process and creating the game’s promotional trailer. I collaborated with a team of designers and engineers throughout the development process.

Siege Chess is an asymmetric strategy board game inspired by chess, where one player commands an attacking force attempting to breach a fortified wall while the other controls defensive towers and obstacles to stop them. I designed the game’s asymmetric mechanics, unit and tower abilities, movement and combat systems, and overall ruleset. I created a digital prototype in Tabletop Simulator to test and refine the gameplay, as well as a physical version using 3D printing and laser cutting. As a solo project, I was responsible for the complete design, prototyping, testing, and physical production of the game.

Sheet Happens is an experimental crossword game that uses Google Sheets as its game engine. I designed and developed the game independently, creating its mechanics and systems entirely within Google Sheets as a fun experiment and a way to further develop my skills with a tool I regularly use in my professional work. The project challenged me to explore unconventional ways of implementing game logic, interactions, and systems within the limitations of a spreadsheet.

Queen of the Anthill is a two-player arcade game where the players control a colony of ants, trying to defend it from incoming bugs that threaten the anthill. My responsibilities included designing the overall game loop and defining the core player interactions and mechanics. I also designed and built a custom functional arcade controller tailored specifically to the game, combining physical controls with the digital gameplay. I collaborated with a team of Artists, Designer, and Developers to integrate the controller and gameplay systems into the final arcade experience.

Traffic Madness is a fast-paced arcade game where players navigate increasingly challenging traffic situations while trying to avoid collisions. I designed the core game loop and all of the game’s levels, focusing on creating an engaging and progressively challenging experience. The game was developed in Godot as a solo project in less than 10 days as part of a Game Jam, requiring rapid iteration and efficient implementation of the core gameplay experience.

Flappy Bird 3D is an endless runner inspired by the classic Flappy Bird gameplay, where players navigate a flapping character through an endless series of pipes. This project was created as a personal exercise to learn and gain practical experience with Unreal Engine. My work was primarily technical, focusing on implementing the core gameplay systems.

Pac-Man 3D is an endless runner, where players have to collect pellets and dodge ghosts. This project was created as a personal exercise to learn and gain practical experience with the Godot Engine. My work was primarily technical, focusing on implementing the core gameplay systems.
